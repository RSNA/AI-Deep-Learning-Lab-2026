This hands-on lab moves past research metrics to what matters at deployment: data shift, data drift and model drift, generalizability, uncertainty and clinical utility. You will:

🔹 Define what makes a model ready for clinical deployment, beyond research metrics

🔹 See how radiologist oversight keeps AI safe after go-live
